Intelligent Hiring, End to End

Six AI-powered capabilities that transform your recruiting process from guesswork to precision.

AI Candidate Ranking

Machine learning models analyze resumes, experience, and skills to produce a match score for every applicant — surfacing top talent in seconds, not hours.

Smart Screening

Automated screening questions, resume parsing, and qualification checks filter candidates intelligently. Only the most relevant reach your inbox.

Pipeline Analytics

Real-time dashboards track time-to-hire, drop-off rates, source effectiveness, and funnel conversion. Make data-driven recruiting decisions.
