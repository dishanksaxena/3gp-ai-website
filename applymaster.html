Core Features

Your Complete Job Application Arsenal

Six AI-powered tools that automate your entire job search from discovery to offer.

🚀

Auto Apply

Set your preferences once and ApplyMaster applies to matching jobs 24/7. It fills out forms, uploads documents, and answers screening questions using your profile.

📄

Resume Tailoring

AI rewrites and optimizes your resume for each specific job posting. Keywords, skills, and experience are reordered to maximize ATS scores and recruiter attention.

Cover Letter AI

Generate compelling, personalized cover letters in seconds. Each letter is uniquely crafted to match the company culture, role requirements, and your experience.

Real-time dashboard tracks every application status. See who viewed your resume, interview invitations, and follow-up reminders — all in one place.

Interview Prep

Smart Job Matching

Machine learning analyzes your skills, experience, and preferences to find jobs you are most likely to land. Stop wasting time on long-shot applications.
