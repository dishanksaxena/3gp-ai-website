Our Story

Founded on a Simple Belief

3GP.AI was born from a frustration shared by builders everywhere: too many AI products look great in demos but fall apart in production. We set out to change that.

Our founding team came together from enterprise engineering, infrastructure, and applied machine learning backgrounds. We had seen firsthand how organizations struggle to move from AI experiments to real, production-grade systems that drive revenue.

So we built 3GP.AI around a single principle: AI should deliver real business outcomes, not just impressive demos. Every product in our portfolio is designed to work at enterprise scale, integrate with existing workflows, and prove its value through measurable results.
